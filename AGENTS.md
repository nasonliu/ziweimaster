# ZiweiMaster Agent Rules

## Ziwei Analysis Grounding

- For any Ziwei Dou Shu reading, business/life forecast, timing judgment, partner analysis, employee analysis, community analysis, or related strategic suggestion, ground the answer in ZiweiMaster chart data first.
- Do not provide generic business, psychological, or strategy advice as the main basis. Call ZiweiMaster tools first when chart data, palace inspection, decade, annual fortune, Sanfang Sizheng, or transformation flow is relevant.
- Explicitly connect conclusions to returned chart evidence such as palace names, stars and brightness, natal/decade/annual layers, Sanfang Sizheng, heavenly stems, palace-stem transformations, Lu/Quan/Ke/Ji, opposite-palace impact, and limit/year overlays.
- When giving North-school style analysis, trace the energy flow in plain language: source palace/stem -> transformed star/type -> target palace -> opposite palace impact -> Sanfang Sizheng support or pressure -> likely manifestation.
- If the chart output does not contain the required star, palace, transformation, or timing evidence, say the current Ziwei evidence does not provide it instead of filling the gap with unsupported reasoning.
- Practical recommendations are allowed only after the Ziwei basis has been established, and should be framed as applications of the chart reading rather than independent claims.
